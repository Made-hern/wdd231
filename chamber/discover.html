A City Full of History and Culture

Antigua Guatemala is a historic city known for its colonial architecture, cobblestone streets, cultural traditions, and beautiful views of surrounding volcanoes. Its heritage and vibrant community make it an attractive place to visit and do business.

Our heritage

A City with a Rich History

Antigua Guatemala was founded in the 16th century and served as the capital of the Kingdom of Guatemala. After the earthquakes of 1773, the capital was moved to its present location. Today, Antigua is recognized for its preserved colonial architecture and historic urban layout.

In 1979, Antigua Guatemala was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Local traditions

Culture and Events

Antigua is known for its religious traditions, cultural festivals, local markets, and artistic activities. Holy Week is one of the city's most important annual celebrations, featuring processions and colorful street carpets.

The Festival of Flowers is another cultural event that brings color and visitors to the historic center.
